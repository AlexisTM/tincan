Tincan: Private P2P Chat

Private chat straight between devices. No account, no phone number, no ads.

---

Tincan is a chat app with no account and no server in the middle. Your phone talks straight to your friend's, encrypted end to end.

Chat
• Texts, emoji, photos and videos, with replies and reactions
• Disappearing messages, per conversation
• See whether a chat is direct or relayed

Connect
• Your Tincan ID is all anyone needs: no phone number, no email
• Share it as a QR code, as text or as a contact card
• First contact is a request: accept it, ignore it or block it for good
• Compare safety numbers to know you are talking to the right person

Stay private
• Encrypted end to end, straight between devices when your networks allow it, otherwise through a relay that can't read your messages
• Everything on your device is stored encrypted, unlocked with your password or your fingerprint
• Make a new Tincan ID to cut off everyone holding the old one
• Back up your account and chats to move to a new device

Nothing to sign up for, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. On Android and Linux.
